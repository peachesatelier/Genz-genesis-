A first collection about identity, movement, experimentation and the energy of a new generation. Fashion as a beginning — made in Lagos, imagined beyond it.

The
Campaign

Nine images from the GENZ GENESIS campaign, moving between colour, tailoring, pearls, romance and raw attitude.

A new
beginning.

GENZ GENESIS is the first chapter of Peaches Atelier Studio: a study of youth, craft and self-expression. The collection moves between softness and structure, classic references and contemporary attitude. Each look treats clothing as both garment and image — something to be worn, photographed, remembered and pushed forward.
